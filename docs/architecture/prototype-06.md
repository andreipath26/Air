# Prototype 06 — consolidated acquisition and explicit causal/probabilistic reasoning
Atomic batches of up to 128 facts normalise through operator-approved aliases for all triple positions and categories. The original source strings remain; aliases are not automatically learned. Package manifests verify SHA-256 of input before transaction, with a 1 MiB limit; declared license is metadata only, not legally validated. Hashes establish content integrity relative to supplied digest, not authenticity or truth. Source quote checks independently reproduce anchoring from original bytes; they do not establish entailment. Rejected claims are excluded from integrated session output. Reviewed causes edges support bounded cycle-safe path exploration; causal chains are not interventions or proof of causality. Bayesian update operates on explicitly supplied priors and likelihoods under conditional independence; no probabilities are invented from source count. Tests cover numerical posterior, alias merging, source integrity and causal traversal. No general autonomous semantic consolidation, learned causal discovery, calibrated probabilities, compressed large-scale store, whole-system resource limit or hardware validation is claimed.
